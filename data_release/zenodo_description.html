Grid and format

  • 72,632 columns x 55,084 rows, EPSG:4326 (WGS 84), pixel 0.000269494585 degrees (nominal 30 m resolution); upper-left corner 115.5218184 E, 53.5636658 N; extent 115.52-135.10 E, 38.72-53.56 N. All six files share this grid exactly. Actual pixel area varies with latitude and is calculated on the WGS 84 ellipsoid.
  • Int16; stored value = 10 x AGB in Mg ha-1 (scale factor 0.1 is written in the band metadata; GDAL tools, QGIS, R terra and ArcGIS apply it; in rasterio / numpy multiply by 0.1 yourself). Precision 0.1 Mg ha-1; the largest difference to the Float32 originals is 0.05 Mg ha-1 and the pixel arithmetic means agree to better than 1e-5 Mg ha-1.
  • NoData = -32768: outside the forest mask (and, in the AGB maps, source pixels <= 0).
  • Cloud-optimised GeoTIFF: DEFLATE (level 9, predictor 2), 512 x 512 tiles, overviews (average resampling, factors 2-256). The files can be read in place over HTTP with /vsicurl/ or opened in QGIS without downloading when the host supports range requests.
  • Tags in each file: TITLE, YEAR, UNITS, SCALE_FACTOR, NODATA_VALUE, VALUE_RULE, FOREST_MASK, MODEL, SOURCE_FILE, CRS, CREATED, REFERENCE. Existing TIFFs contain a descriptive CRS tag stating "about 30 m, 0.09 ha". This is a legacy nominal-resolution description, not the actual area of each geographic-grid pixel; do not use 0.09 ha for regional area or stock calculations. The raster CRS, transform and pixel values are unchanged.

Definitions

  • AGB: aboveground biomass of living trees, dry mass, Mg ha-1 (oven-dry tonnes per hectare).
  • Forest mask: China Annual Tree Cover Dataset (CATCD; Cai et al. 2024) tree cover > 10 % of the same year. After applying this mask, forest area is operationally defined as the summed WGS 84 ellipsoidal area of valid, finite pixels with AGB > 0. Pixels outside the mask, NoData and AGB <= 0 are excluded. The resulting forest areas are 41.54 Mha in 2005, 55.75 Mha in 2015 and 58.68 Mha in 2024 (Table 2 of the paper).
  • Change maps: AGB of the later year minus AGB of the earlier year, over the union of the forest pixels of both years, as drawn in Figure 5 d-f; positive = gain. A pixel that is forest in only one of the two years enters with AGB = 0 for the year in which it is not forest, so new forest shows its full later-year AGB as a gain and cleared forest its full earlier-year AGB as a loss.
  • Regional statistics of the paper (Table 2, Figure 6): calculated from the Float32 RF source maps using the same mask and AGB > 0 rule. For valid pixel i with AGB density B_i (Mg ha-1) and WGS 84 ellipsoidal area a_i (ha), forest area = sum(a_i), area-weighted mean AGB density = sum(B_i x a_i) / sum(a_i), and AGB carbon stock = sum(B_i x a_i) x 0.50 / 1e9 (Pg C; carbon fraction 0.50). Pixel areas are calculated between the latitude boundaries of each raster row. The values for Northeast China are:

Method summary

Field plots compiled from the literature (two periods, 2005-2010 and 2011-2015) were matched with growing-season Landsat composites, PALSAR / PALSAR-2 mosaics and SRTM terrain; nine model / data combinations (RF, Cubist, MLR x Landsat, PALSAR, both) were compared over 100 random training / validation splits; the RF model on Landsat + PALSAR was used for the maps. The code of every figure and of the model workflow, including the scripts that built and checked the files of this record, is in the GitHub repository https://github.com/Yu-xuan-Zhou/NEChina-AGB-30m (MIT License).

Processing

The maps were exported from Google Earth Engine as Float32 GeoTIFF (masked with CATCD) and converted with build_release_maps.py: values multiplied by 10 and rounded to Int16, NaN (and, for the AGB maps, values <= 0) set to NoData, written as cloud-optimised GeoTIFF with gdal_translate (GDAL 3.6.2).
